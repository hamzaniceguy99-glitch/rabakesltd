Terms of service

What we provide

Rabakes provides food production and bakery services provided by Rabakes Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 13834508. The business is registered as a food business with its local authority under registration [FOOD BUSINESS REGISTRATION DETAILS] and operates a food safety management system based on hazard analysis; the food hygiene rating issued following local authority inspection is available on request and on the Food Standards Agency website. A full ingredient list, with the 14 allergens required to be declared emphasised, is supplied with every order. IMPORTANT: the kitchen handles nuts, peanuts, milk, eggs, cereals containing gluten, soya and sesame, and products are made in a shared environment, so cross-contact cannot be excluded and no product can be described or supplied as free from any allergen; anyone with a severe allergy or anaphylaxis risk should not order and should be advised accordingly. Where a product is prepacked for direct sale it is labelled with the product name and full ingredient list with allergens emphasised, as Natasha's Law requires. Cakes containing non-edible supports, dowels, wires, floral picks or decorations are identified on delivery and must be removed before serving. Deposits for celebration and wedding orders are non-refundable on the schedule stated in the written quotation. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.

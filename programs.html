Program 01 · From a conversation

Celebration cakes

Tell us the occasion, the number of people and what you want to spend, and we will design to that rather than quoting a catalogue picture. We are also honest that elaborate sugar work costs hours rather than ingredients, so the price reflects time more than size.

  • Everyone
  • Quoted per cake

Program 02 · Tasting before deciding

Wedding cakes

Tasting comes first, because choosing a flavour from a photograph is how people end up with a cake they do not like. Quotation is written, deposits are non-refundable on a stated schedule, and we plan delivery around the venue — summer marquees and buttercream are a known problem we will warn you about.

  • Book months ahead
  • Couples
  • Quoted in writing
  • Deposit to hold
